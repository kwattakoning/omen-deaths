# Omen Deaths

A death leaderboard for the guild **&lt;Omen&gt;** in World of Warcraft: Forever.
Who dies the most? Who keeps falling off Thunder Bluff? Omen Deaths keeps score, shares it with
the guild and posts a funny line in guild chat for every death.

## Download

### [⬇ Download the newest version (OmenDeaths.zip)](https://github.com/kwattakoning/omen-deaths/releases/latest/download/OmenDeaths.zip)

This link always gives the newest version. The addon tells you in-game when a new one is out.

## What it does

- Counts every death and shares it with everyone in the guild who has the addon
- Leaderboard for Today, This Week, This Month and All Time, per place: Everywhere, Open World,
  Dungeons, Raids (each raid on its own) and Battlegrounds
- All Time also counts the deaths from before you installed the addon (from the game's own statistics)
- Killers: which mobs (and falls) kill us the most
- A funny line in guild chat when you die. The "Guild chat" button has three settings:
  **No BG** (default: not for deaths in battlegrounds), **Off** and **On**
- One summary in guild chat per boss fight instead of a line per death
- Only shows players who are in the guild now

Open it with `/omen` or the minimap button.

## Install

1. Close World of Warcraft.
2. Unzip the download. You get one folder called `OmenDeaths`.
3. Put that folder in `World of Warcraft\_forever_\Interface\AddOns\`.
   It must end up as `...\AddOns\OmenDeaths\OmenDeaths.toc`
   (not `...\AddOns\OmenDeaths\OmenDeaths\...`).

Updating works the same way: delete the old `OmenDeaths` folder and put the new one in its place,
then restart the game (a /reload is not always enough).
Your deaths are saved elsewhere, so you keep them.

More help: `HOW-TO-INSTALL.txt` inside the zip.
