# Omen Deaths

A death leaderboard for the guild **&lt;Omen&gt;** in World of Warcraft: Forever.
Who dies the most? Who keeps falling off Thunder Bluff? Omen Deaths keeps score, shares it with
the guild and posts a funny line in guild chat for every death.

## Download

**Easiest: [Omen Deaths on CurseForge](https://www.curseforge.com/wow/addons/omendeaths).**
Install it in the CurseForge app (World of Warcraft → Forever) and updates go automatically.

Or by hand: [⬇ Download the newest version (OmenDeaths.zip)](https://github.com/kwattakoning/omen-deaths/releases/latest/download/OmenDeaths.zip)
This link always gives the newest version. The addon tells you in-game when a new one is out.

## What it does

- Counts every death and shares it with everyone in the guild who has the addon
- Leaderboard for Today, This Week, This Month and All Time, per place: Everywhere, Open World,
  Dungeons, Raids (each raid and each raid boss on its own) and Battlegrounds
- All Time also counts the deaths from before you installed the addon (from the game's own statistics)
- Killers: which mobs (and falls) kill us the most
- A funny line in guild chat when you die, fitting how you died: a fall, lava, your role in a dungeon,
  a mob way above your level or a gray one. Die the same way again and it gets worse.
  The "Guild chat" button switches it **On** (default) or **Off**. Battleground and arena deaths are never posted
  (and give no popup or sound), but they still count under Battlegrounds
- Special lines for milestones (your 100th death!), dying together, a mob on a killing spree, the end of a
  clean streak, dying right after a level-up and being one-shot, plus jokes for every class
- Last week's awards in guild chat after the weekly reset: Death of the week, Floor Inspector, Deadliest foe
- One summary in guild chat per boss fight instead of a line per death
- Auto repair at merchants (on by default) and a Repairs board: who pays the biggest repair bills
- Optional sound when a guildie dies: the human death scream, male or female
- Only shows players who are in the guild now

Open it with `/omen` or the minimap button.

## Install

With the CurseForge app: pick World of Warcraft, then **Forever**, search for *Omen Deaths* and click Install.

By hand:

1. Close World of Warcraft.
2. Unzip the download. You get one folder called `OmenDeaths`.
3. Put that folder in `World of Warcraft\_forever_\Interface\AddOns\`.
   It must end up as `...\AddOns\OmenDeaths\OmenDeaths.toc`
   (not `...\AddOns\OmenDeaths\OmenDeaths\...`).

Updating works the same way: delete the old `OmenDeaths` folder and put the new one in its place,
then restart the game (a /reload is not always enough).
Your deaths are saved elsewhere, so you keep them.

### Switching from the GitHub download to CurseForge

Just install it in the CurseForge app; it replaces the old folder. Your deaths are saved elsewhere,
so you keep them. From then on the app keeps it up to date.

More help: `HOW-TO-INSTALL.txt` inside the zip.
