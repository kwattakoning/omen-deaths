=====================================================================
  OMEN DEATHS  -  the <Omen> Hall of the Fallen
  A death leaderboard for our guild in World of Warcraft: Forever
=====================================================================

Who dies the most? Who keeps losing to Hogger? Who tested gravity
one time too many? Omen Deaths keeps score, shares it with the guild,
and makes fun of every death in guild chat.


---------------------------------------------------------------------
 1. INSTALLING
---------------------------------------------------------------------

 1. Close World of Warcraft completely.

 2. Unzip the download. You get a folder called "OmenDeaths".
    (The folder name must stay exactly "OmenDeaths".)

 3. Put that folder in your WoW Forever AddOns folder:

      C:\Program Files (x86)\World of Warcraft\_forever_\Interface\AddOns\

    The result must look like this:

      ...\_forever_\Interface\AddOns\OmenDeaths\OmenDeaths.toc

    Tip: if you see ...\AddOns\OmenDeaths\OmenDeaths\..., you have one
    folder too many. Move the inner folder up one level.

    Can't find the folder? Open the Battle.net app, pick WoW Forever,
    click the gear icon next to "Play" and choose "Show in Explorer".
    Then go to Interface\AddOns. (If there is no "Interface" or
    "AddOns" folder yet, just create it.)

 4. Start WoW. On the character select screen click "AddOns" (bottom
    left) and check that "Omen Deaths" is ticked. If it says "out of
    date", tick "Load out of date AddOns" at the top.

 5. Log in. You'll see a little skull button on the edge of your
    minimap. That's it!

 Updating: the newest version is always at
   https://github.com/kwattakoning/omen-deaths/releases/latest/download/OmenDeaths.zip
 Close WoW, delete the old OmenDeaths folder, put the new
 one in its place. Your saved deaths are not in this folder, so you
 keep them.

 Uninstalling: close WoW and delete the OmenDeaths folder.


---------------------------------------------------------------------
 2. HOW IT WORKS
---------------------------------------------------------------------

 * Every member who runs the addon counts their OWN deaths and shares
   them over the hidden guild addon channel. Nothing is typed in chat
   for this; other guildies' addons pick it up automatically.

 * Only guildies who have the addon installed show up on the
   leaderboard. (The game does not let addons see other players' deaths
   any other way.) So: get everyone to install it!

 * Logged in later? The addon catches up by itself: it fetches the
   deaths that happened while you were offline from guildies who are
   online.

 * Deaths from BEFORE you installed the addon: the "All Time" board uses
   the game's own statistic (Achievements > Statistics > Deaths >
   Total deaths), which counts from your character's first day. So
   your full lifetime count shows up there right away.

 * Your pet dying does not count. Feign Death does not count.
   Only a real death of your own character counts.


---------------------------------------------------------------------
 3. THE WINDOW
---------------------------------------------------------------------

 Open it by clicking the skull on the minimap (or type /omen).
 Close it with the X, with Escape, or by clicking the skull again.
 Drag the window by its edge to move it. Drag the minimap skull to
 move it around the minimap.

 Hover over the minimap skull to see this week's #1 and your own count.

 TABS (top row)
   Today        deaths since the last daily reset
   This Week    deaths since the last weekly reset (= your raid week)
   This Month   deaths this calendar month
   All Time     everything, including the game's lifetime statistic

 VIEWS
   The Fallen   players, most deaths first. Gold, silver and bronze for
                the top 3, and everyone gets a (not so) honorary title,
                like "Spirit Healer's Favourite" or "Floor Inspector".
   Killers      what killed us the most: mobs, bosses, falling, lava...
                "Unknown causes" = the game didn't tell us the killer.
                "Before Omen Deaths" = deaths from the game's statistic,
                from before the addon was installed (no killer known).
   Health       how the addon is doing for every guildie who runs it:
                their version ("old" = time to update), deaths the game
                gave no cause for, and errors in the addon. Hover a row
                for the latest error. Handy when someone reports a bug.

 PLACE  ( <  Everywhere  > )
   Use the arrows to switch between:
     Everywhere      all deaths except battlegrounds and arenas
     Open World      outside dungeons and raids
     Dungeons        5-player dungeons
     All Raids       every raid
     Battlegrounds   battlegrounds and arenas
     <raid name>     every raid someone has died in gets its own board,
                     e.g. "Molten Core"

 HOVER over a player to see their nemesis (what killed them most), and
 when, where, by what and at which level they last died.
 HOVER over a killer to see all its victims.

 The bottom lines show the total for the current view, the deadliest
 foe, your own count, and how many guildies are online with the addon.


---------------------------------------------------------------------
 4. THE BUTTONS AT THE BOTTOM
---------------------------------------------------------------------

 Popup: On/Off
   A banner at the top of your screen when a guildie (or you) dies,
   with a funny line, the killer, the place and the death count of the
   week. Click the banner to open the leaderboard. Only you see it.

 My chat: On/Off
   The same message as one line in your own chat window, so you can
   read it back later. Only you see it.
   (With "Guild chat" on as well, you may see a death twice. Switch
   this one off if that bothers you.)

 Guild chat: On/Off   (on by default)
   When YOU die, the addon posts one funny line in guild chat, so the
   whole guild can laugh, also those without the addon. For example:

     [Omen Deaths] Hogger added Benny to their trophy wall - by Rend in
     Elwynn Forest. Deaths this week: 3

   The line fits how you died: falling, drowning, lava, a dungeon, a
   raid boss, a battleground, a mob in the open world...
   Only the person who died posts it, so there are no double lines.


---------------------------------------------------------------------
 5. BOSS FIGHTS
---------------------------------------------------------------------

 No chat spam during a wipe: deaths during a boss fight don't get a
 line each. Instead, a few seconds after the fight ONE guildie posts a
 summary (the addons agree among themselves who does it):

   [Omen Deaths] Wipe at Lucifron! 12 brave souls fell: Benny, ...
   [Omen Deaths] Magmadar is down! It only cost 1 life: Benny

 A clean kill without deaths gets no message. During boss fights the
 game holds back addon messages; everything is sent right after the
 fight, nothing gets lost.


---------------------------------------------------------------------
 6. QUESTIONS
---------------------------------------------------------------------

 I don't see the skull on my minimap.
   Check that the folder is in the right place (step 3) and that the
   addon is ticked in the AddOns list (step 4). A brand-new addon needs
   a full restart of the game; /reload is not enough the first time.

 A guildie is missing from the board.
   They need the addon too. Once they install it, their deaths are
   counted from then on, and their "All Time" shows their full count.

 My numbers differ between "All Time" and "This Month".
   That's normal: All Time includes the game's lifetime statistic,
   the other tabs only count deaths seen since the addon was installed.

 Can I turn the guild chat messages off?
   Yes: click "Guild chat: On" in the window to make it "Off".

 Does this addon do anything in combat?
   No. It only notices that you died and shares that. It never casts,
   targets or decides anything for you.

 Can't open the window by clicking?
   Type /omen in chat (or /omendeaths).
